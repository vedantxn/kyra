# Mission

This repository exists to help me earn the opportunity to work with Kyra and contribute meaningfully to the company Sahil Dhull is building.

The goal is not simply to get hired. It is to do thoughtful, high-quality work that demonstrates how I think, how I build, and the standard I would bring to Kyra as an early engineer. If I get the opportunity, I want to help build Kyra into an exceptional and enduring company.

## Why Kyra

I want to work with Sahil because he has the kind of experience and judgment I want to learn from. He studied at IIT Kanpur, spent four years at Cohesity in San Francisco, and had a previous startup accepted into a16z Speedrun before leaving to build Kyra.

He is clearly intelligent and capable, but what stands out even more to me is his humility. That combination matters: strong experience, ambition, technical depth, and the humility to build with other people.

I am deliberately looking for a serious early-stage team led by someone who has already spent years building and learning - not a company driven mainly by social-media attention or startup hype. I believe Sahil has the experience and character to make Kyra a significant company, and I want to be part of the incredible team he is assembling from the beginning.

## Motivation

My motivation is to contribute, learn quickly, and become the kind of engineer an ambitious early-stage company can rely on. Everything in this repository should move toward that purpose: understand Kyra deeply, identify where I can create real value, and prove it through the quality of my work.
